Terms

Soul Scribe is a private, invitation-only hobby site run by its admin for a group of friends. Use it as a decent person would: don't share your invite, don't try to access other people's accounts, and don't abuse the game data services it relies on.

The site is provided as-is, for free, with no guarantees that it will keep working, that stats are accurate, or that your data will be retained. Game data comes from the community deadlock-api.com project and from Valve's Deadlock; Soul Scribe is not affiliated with or endorsed by Valve.

The admin can revoke access at any time.
