Privacy

Soul Scribe is a private site for a group of friends who play Deadlock. It is not affiliated with Valve.

What we store. Your Google email (to know who you are), your public Steam account ID and display name (to find your matches), your Deadlock match history as published by the community deadlock-api.com project, and any builds you save here.

What we never see. Your Steam or Google passwords. Sign-in is handled by Steam and Google directly.

The uploader. The optional match uploader sends only match IDs, their replay keys and your public Steam ID to deadlock-api.com so your matches can be found. It never sends your Steam login.

Sharing. Your stats and builds are visible to the other invited members of this site, and to no one else. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Removal. Ask the site admin to remove your account and everything tied to it is deleted.
