Get set up

Three steps, about two minutes. Soul Scribe tracks your Deadlock games, shows the squad leaderboard, and keeps your builds.

1

Sign in through Steam

This tells us which Steam account is yours. We never see your password; Steam hands back only your public ID. Your match history imports straight away.

2

Install the match uploader

Valve doesn't publish match lists. A game only reaches Soul Scribe once someone in it uploads it, so this small background tool does that for you. One upload covers all 12 players in the game.

Download the match uploader (.exe) The open-source deadlock-api-ingest tool.

  1. Run the downloaded deadlock-api-ingest-setup.exe. If Windows warns, choose More info → Run anyway. Already installed? Run it again to update. Versions older than 0.4.6 miss most games.
  2. When you sign in to Steam, tick Remember me. While Deadlock is closed, the uploader uses that saved sign-in to ask Steam for games you and your Steam friends played that haven't been uploaded yet.
  3. Leave it running after you quit the game. It starts on login and catches up every 30 minutes.
Missing older games? Run this once in PowerShell to recover them from Steam:
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/deadlock-api/deadlock-api-ingest/master/fetch-own-matches.ps1 | iex

Squad tip: add each other as Steam friends. Anyone running the uploader then also picks up their friends' games, so the whole squad stays covered.

Sends match IDs and match keys, your Steam ID and your Steam friends' IDs. Your Steam password and saved sign-in never leave your PC. Open source.

3

Play, then check the leaderboard

The site re-syncs everyone hourly, so uploaded games show up within the hour. Your profile shows your rank, heroes and matches; Builds is where you keep and share builds.
