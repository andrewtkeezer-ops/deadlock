Get set up

Three steps, about two minutes. Soul Scribe tracks your Deadlock games, shows the squad leaderboard, and keeps your builds.

1

Sign in through Steam

This tells us which Steam account is yours. We never see your password; Steam hands back only your public ID. Your match history imports straight away.

2

Install the match uploader

Valve doesn't publish match lists. A tracker needs each match's ID plus a key only your Steam client receives, so a small background tool sends them as you play and backfills your old games.

Download the Soul Scribe uploader (.exe, ~32 MB) Built from the open-source deadlock-api-ingest with squad extras: instant leaderboard refresh, catches games you forgot to open, publishes your builds into the game.

  1. Run soul-scribe-setup.exe. If Windows warns, choose More info → Run anyway.
  2. It installs for your user only and starts on login. Close the status window; it keeps running.
  3. Play. New games appear within minutes; older games backfill over a day or so.

Sends only match IDs and your public Steam ID, never your Steam login. Open source.

3

Play, then check the leaderboard

The site re-syncs everyone hourly, and the uploader nudges it right after each game. Your profile shows your rank, heroes and matches; Builds is where you keep and share builds.
