Get your matches into Soul Scribe

Valve doesn't publish a list of matches. A tracker needs each match's ID plus a one-time key (a "salt") that only your Steam client downloads. So every friend runs a tiny background tool once; after that, every game you play shows up in Soul Scribe automatically, and it backfills your old games too.

Step 1 — install the match uploader (Windows)

Download installer (.exe, ~32 MB)

  1. Run deadlock-api-ingest-setup.exe. Windows SmartScreen may warn because it's a small open-source project: click More info → Run anyway.
  2. It installs to your user folder and starts on login. A small window lists each match it sends; close it, it keeps running in the background.
  3. Play Deadlock. Your new games are sent as you play, and old ones backfill over the next day or so.

It only sends match IDs and your public Steam ID, never your Steam login. Open source.

Prefer a command line, or on Linux / Steam Deck?

Windows PowerShell:

irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/deadlock-api/deadlock-api-ingest/master/install-windows.ps1 | iex

Linux:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/deadlock-api/deadlock-api-ingest/master/install-linux.sh | bash

Only while the game runs: download deadlock-api-ingest-windows-latest.exe from the releases page and set Deadlock's Steam launch option to "C:\Users\YOU\AppData\Local\deadlock-api-ingest\deadlock-api-ingest.exe" -- %command%

Step 2 — sign in through Steam

This just tells us which Steam account is yours. We never see your password; Steam only hands back your public ID. Your matches import right away.
